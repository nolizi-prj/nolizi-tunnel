## 2. Coherence and Internal Consistency

- **State Disambiguation ([`SPEC.md` §2](file:///home/m/dev/pumasi-tunnel/spec/0004-names-with-owners/SPEC.md#L20-L41)):**  
  The specification clearly distinguishes `registered` (active tunnel in `core.Registry`), `reserved` (exclusive entitlement in `core.Reservations`), and `durable` (disk-backed persistence in Slice 2). `Registry.Register` continues to refuse live duplicate names with `ErrNameTaken`, preserving active connection exclusion independently of ownership.
- **Cryptographic & Token Model ([`SPEC.md` §3.1, §5.1](file:///home/m/dev/pumasi-tunnel/spec/0004-names-with-owners/SPEC.md#L65-L80)):**  
  Storing `sha256(token)` in hex with `subtle.ConstantTimeCompare` is soundly justified given the strict `MinTokenLen = 16` enforcement. Short tokens fail fast with `ErrTokenTooShort` upon `Claim`, while `Check` treats short tokens as absent tokens.
- **Port Holding & Pool Semantics ([`SPEC.md` §5.2](file:///home/m/dev/pumasi-tunnel/spec/0004-names-with-owners/SPEC.md#L157-L179)):**  
  Introducing the `held` state to `core.PortPool` and keying ownership by subdomain rather than ephemeral `agentID` ensures that re-dialing agents can reclaim their allocated ports. Generic `Allocate` skips held ports, while `AllocateSpecific` succeeds only for the designated holder.
- **Two-Path Ingress Coverage ([`lessons/L-009`](file:///home/m/dev/governance/lessons/L-009-two-paths-one-claim.md), [`SPEC.md` §5.3](file:///home/m/dev/pumasi-tunnel/spec/0004-names-with-owners/SPEC.md#L180-L204)):**  
  Both agent ingress (`ServeAgent`) and SSH ingress (`ServeSSH`) share a single authorization checkpoint in `Relay.authorize`, ensuring consistent reservation enforcement across both transport paths.
