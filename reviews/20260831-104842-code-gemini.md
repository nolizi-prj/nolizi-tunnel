### 2.2 Single Source of Truth ([L-007](file:///home/m/dev/pumasi/lessons/L-007-restating-a-rule-forks-it.md))
- [`core.Registry.PublicURL`](file:///home/m/dev/pumasi-tunnel/core/route.go#L311) is the sole constructor of tunnel URLs:
  $$PublicURL(N) = S + \text{"://"} + \text{lower}(N) + \text{"."} + D$$
- All three user-facing surfaces consume this single source of truth without constructing their own URLs:
  1. **Surface 1 (CLI Output):** [`r.registry.PublicURL(name)`](file:///home/m/dev/pumasi-tunnel/relay/relay.go#L255) in [`relay.authorize`](file:///home/m/dev/pumasi-tunnel/relay/relay.go#L225).
  2. **Surface 2 (Apex Console Status):** [`r.registry.PublicURL(t.Subdomain)`](file:///home/m/dev/pumasi-tunnel/relay/dashboard.go#L71) in [`relay.serveStatus`](file:///home/m/dev/pumasi-tunnel/relay/dashboard.go#L63).
  3. **Surface 3 (Zero-Install SSH Banner):** Consumes `resp.URL` via [`relay.ServeSSH`](file:///home/m/dev/pumasi-tunnel/relay/sshingress.go#L190).
- Raw TCP address formatting ([`resp.TCPAddr`](file:///home/m/dev/pumasi-tunnel/relay/relay.go#L267)) remains untouched as `host:port` without a scheme prefix.
